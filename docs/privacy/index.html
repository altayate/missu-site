2. Who receives information

  • Your connected partner: your displayed name, shared photos and titles, Daily photos and reactions, drawings, and the connection information needed for the experience. They may save, screenshot, or otherwise copy what you share.
  • Google/Firebase: authentication, database, file storage, backend processing, push delivery, app integrity checks, analytics, crash reporting, and AdMob when used. Read Firebase’s privacy information, Google’s privacy policy, and AdMob’s data disclosures.
  • Apple: sign-in, app integrity services, push delivery, purchases, and subscription management. See Apple’s privacy policy.
  • GitHub: hosts this static website and may receive technical request information, such as your IP address. See GitHub’s privacy statement. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising, cookies, or a contact form to this website.

We do not sell your photos, drawings, or account information. We do not provide your shared photo or drawing content to advertising providers. Service providers may process the technical information described above under their applicable terms. Information may also be disclosed when legally required, to address fraud or security threats, or in a business transfer subject to applicable privacy protections.

Content reports

When you report a partner’s gallery or Daily photo, or a shared drawing, you explicitly agree to send Miss-U a copy of that content, your reason and optional note, and the relevant account and content identifiers for human moderation review. A drawing report includes a snapshot of the shared canvas and may show both partners’ strokes. The app prepares a separate image copy without original photo metadata; it does not share your photo encryption keys. This review copy is outside the normal partner-only photo encryption and is accessible only through restricted administrative systems.

Submitting a report hides the reported content for you and your widgets when they refresh. Reports do not automatically disconnect you, notify your partner, remove the original or suspend an account. Reviewers may dismiss the report, remove violating content or restrict an account. You can separately disconnect through Settings. A reported drawing canvas stays hidden for you until it is cleared or replaced.

Reports and their evidence expire 90 days after submission, even if the original expires, you disconnect or an account is deleted. Scheduled cleanup removes expired active records; an outage or backlog can delay removal. Minimal account-scoped hiding markers, without image, reason or note, remain until account deletion so reported content does not reappear. Provider backup and recovery retention may differ. Contact us for report-related questions, to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or to request deletion. We will assess the request under applicable law and explain any permitted reason for retaining information.

3. Photo encryption and its limits

The current photo-sharing implementation encrypts new album and Daily photos on your device before upload. Photo keys are protected using keys derived for the connection. Private identity keys stay in the device Keychain; the backend stores encrypted photo payloads and public keys. You can compare the full photo safety code with your partner through a trusted channel in Settings.

This protection applies to normal photo sharing. If content is reported, the separate moderation review copy can be read by authorized reviewers and is outside partner-only photo encryption. Account and relationship information, file metadata, titles, timestamps, drawings, reactions, and notifications are not covered by that photo encryption. Older uploads may remain unencrypted until the owner’s updated app migrates them. Both partners need compatible updated apps for encrypted sharing.

The app and widgets keep decrypted copies on the device to display images offline, with iOS file protection where configured. Someone who can access your unlocked device or view your widgets may see them. Offline devices cannot be remotely wiped; they learn about deletions or disconnection when they reconnect. iOS controls when a previously rendered widget refreshes. Exported copies cannot be recalled. No storage or transmission method guarantees absolute security.

4. How long information stays

  • Account and album data: generally kept while your account and relevant sharing features are in use, until you delete content, disconnect, or delete your account. Disconnecting queues deletion of both partners’ shared album and Daily photos and related records, and revokes shared drawing access. It is more than simply hiding the other person’s gallery.
  • Daily photos: expire at the first 00:00 UTC after their server creation time. Access is restricted at expiry; background cleanup removes active images and photo/reaction records. A backlog, network issue, or service failure can delay physical removal. Minimal quota and retry records can remain without photo or reaction content.
  • Drawings: the active canvas is stored for synchronization. Clearing or replacing it revokes access to the old session; older session records may remain until backend cleanup or account deletion. Where a daily reset feature is enabled, its displayed deadline and plan rules determine when the active canvas resets. We do not promise that inaccessible historical records are erased instantly.
  • Account deletion: the account is locked, the connection is ended, and background jobs remove the account’s authentication record, profile, device registrations, uploads, related shared content and historical drawing data. The partner’s account remains. Retrying jobs can take longer during a service failure. See the deletion guide.
  • Content reports: the independent 90-day moderation window can continue after content deletion, expiry, disconnection or account deletion. Minimal hiding markers remain until account deletion. See report retention and privacy requests.
  • Recovery copies: the current cloud Storage soft-delete configuration retains deleted object recovery copies for seven days after deletion. Older versions or other provider backup and security records may have separate retention. Removing active data does not immediately erase every recovery copy.
  • Reconnect protection, where enabled: a pseudonymous keyed value derived from your Apple sign-in identity and a reconnect deadline can survive account deletion solely to prevent recreating an account to bypass an existing 24-hour wait. It is scheduled for deletion when the wait expires; retries or an outage can delay cleanup. It does not contain your photos, name, or email.
  • Operational, support, analytics, and purchase records: retained as needed for their stated purposes, configured provider retention, security, purchase verification and restoration, or applicable legal obligations. Account deletion does not automatically erase independent Apple billing records or every provider-held diagnostic record. Contact us for a rights request concerning these records.

5. Your choices and privacy rights

You can stop sharing by disconnecting, delete your own album photos, clear the shared canvas, change system permissions, remove widgets, or request account deletion in Miss-U → Settings → Delete account. Signing out or uninstalling the app alone does not delete your cloud account or cancel an Apple subscription.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a portable copy of your information, object to certain processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, and complain to your local data protection authority. Rights under applicable Turkish data protection law (KVKK) may include learning whether data is processed, its purpose and recipients, requesting correction or lawful erasure, and raising applicable objections. Email altayate@gmail.com. We may need proportionate information to verify your account before acting; do not send Apple passwords or sign-in codes.

Where a legal basis is required, we process data as necessary to provide the requested service, to meet legal obligations, for legitimate interests such as security and service reliability where permitted after considering your rights, or with consent where required. Permission to use the camera or send notifications is managed through iOS; it does not by itself constitute consent to unrelated processing.

6. International processing

Where applicable law requires a transfer mechanism or safeguards, those requirements apply to the transfer. Contact us for information about the safeguards relevant to your data.
