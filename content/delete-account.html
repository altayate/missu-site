Account deletion is permanent. It ends your partner connection and starts removal of your account and associated content. Your partner’s account stays, but shared photos and drawing data can be removed. Save anything important first.

Delete inside the app

  1. Open Miss-U and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings → Delete account.
  3. Read the deletion information and confirm with Continue with Apple, using the same Apple identity.
  4. Wait for the deletion-request confirmation. The app signs you out, and background cleanup continues even if you close it.

If Apple authentication is canceled or the request fails, the app does not show an accepted deletion receipt. Follow the error and try again when online. If you cannot access the app, email {{email}} to request help. We will need to verify your account before deleting it. Never send your Apple password or verification codes.

What is removed?

The deletion process locks your account, ends your connection, invalidates your invitation, and removes your authentication record, profile, device registrations, uploaded files, photo keys stored on the service, photo and Daily records, and related historical drawing data. Shared photo cleanup affects both members of the ended connection. The updated app clears account-related photo keys, pending uploads, downloaded photo files, and widget access on the device processing the deletion.

What may remain temporarily?

  • Background jobs retry if a service fails. Cloud cleanup is not an instant wipe of every copy.
  • Deleted cloud file recovery copies currently remain under a seven-day Storage soft-delete policy.
  • Offline devices and previously rendered widgets may retain content until they receive the change and refresh. Exported photos and screenshots cannot be recalled.
  • Where reconnect protection is enabled, a pseudonymous Apple-identity value and its existing reconnect deadline can remain until the 24-hour wait ends, followed by cleanup. It is used solely to prevent bypassing the wait through account recreation.
  • Apple billing records, limited purchase restoration or fraud-prevention records, support correspondence, and provider diagnostics or legally required records may have separate retention. Ask us about any additional privacy request.

Cancel Apple billing separately

Account deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription. Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Miss-U to manage renewal. See Apple’s cancellation instructions.
