A few common questions

Why isn’t an ad available to unlock drawing?

Ads depend on availability and your applicable privacy choices. If the app shows a privacy-check error, use Retry ad privacy check. Where available, open Settings → Ad privacy choices to review Google’s privacy message. If an ad cannot load, try again later. A production drawing reward starts only after the completed ad is verified by the server; closing an ad before earning its reward does not grant drawing time.

How do we connect?

Sign in using different Apple accounts. In Miss-U’s Settings, one person creates and shares an invitation code; the other enters it. Codes expire after one hour. If a reconnect waiting period is active, wait until the time shown in the app.

Why didn’t a Miss-U notification arrive?

Check Settings → Notifications → Miss-U on the receiving iPhone and allow notifications. Also check Focus modes, connectivity, and that you are still connected in the app. A successful send means the push service accepted the request; device delivery and display can still be delayed.

Why isn’t the widget up to date?

Open Miss-U while online so it can synchronize, then check the widget again. Widgets display local snapshots, and iOS decides when to refresh them. After deletion or disconnection, an offline device or an already rendered widget may keep an older image until the device receives the change and iOS refreshes it.

When do Daily photos disappear?

They expire at the first midnight in UTC (00:00 UTC) after creation, rather than your local midnight. The daily capture allowance and reminder schedule can use a local time zone, so their reset may happen at a different time. Expired photos cannot be restored through the app.

Why can’t I share or open an encrypted photo?

Both partners need compatible updated apps and must open the app to register their photo keys. Follow any encryption message in Settings. Compare the entire photo safety code in person or through a trusted call. Reinstalling or changing devices can affect locally stored keys; contact support before deleting the app if you cannot access important photos.

What happens if we disconnect?

Shared access ends and cleanup is queued for both partners’ shared album and Daily photos. The old drawing is no longer shared. Where enabled, a 24-hour reconnect wait applies to both people, including Premium users. Save anything you want to keep before confirming disconnection.

How do I restore or cancel Premium?

For an available purchase, use Miss-U → Settings → Premium → Restore purchases with the Apple account that made the purchase. A purchase cannot belong to two active Miss-U accounts at once. Manage renewal in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Apple explains cancellation here. For refunds, visit Apple’s refund service. Deleting Miss-U or its account does not cancel billing.

How do I delete my account or request my data?

Use Miss-U → Settings → Delete account and confirm with Apple. Read the deletion guide for what is removed and what may remain temporarily. For access, correction, or other privacy requests, email {{email}}.

How do I report a photo or drawing?

Open a partner’s gallery photo and tap the flag, use the flag beside their current Daily photo, or tap the flag in the shared drawing toolbar when the canvas contains partner content. Choose a reason and optionally add a note, then agree to share the reported image for review. The separate review copy is kept for 90 days. Submitted content is hidden for you, including in widgets when they refresh. Your partner is not notified about the report. You can also disconnect through Settings. Reported drawings stay hidden until the canvas is cleared or replaced. Reporting does not automatically remove the original or restrict an account; review may result in content removal or account restrictions. If your app does not show the flag, update Miss-U or email {{email}} with a description. Do not email illegal imagery or someone else’s intimate photos. For help with a report, a privacy request, or to ask us to review a moderation decision, contact {{email}}. See what report data is collected and retained.
